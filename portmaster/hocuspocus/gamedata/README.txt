The shareware episode (Time Tripping) is unpacked into shareware/ from the
original Apogee package on the first launch.

To play the full game, put the registered HOCUS.DAT and HOCUS.EXE (version
1.1) here or in a registered/ subfolder. The registered data is used from
then on and the shareware files are removed on the next launch; a HOCUS.SAV
made on the shareware episode is moved next to the registered data. HOCUS.SAV
(saves, high scores, options) is written next to the data files in use.
